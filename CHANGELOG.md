# Changelog

Each skill has its own version. A skill keeps its name and its job; 1.0 to 1.1 is an improvement, 2.0 means it works noticeably differently.

## meeting-prep

### 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- The card starts with the person: who they are in one line and what's new with them (a new role, a launch, a hire, a post).
- The opener starts with them, their news or the reason for the call, then a question. Your own intro moved to "If they ask what you do", two sentences.
- Open threads (where things left off) moved up, right after the opener.
- New rules: ask about them first, and on a pricing call write the price as a sentence and say it out loud.
- Deep mode adds "what you thought vs what's true now", a run of show for the call, a floor for the ask, what to say instead for each thing to avoid, and three reasons if they ask why you.
