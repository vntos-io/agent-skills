# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help. I don't merge pull requests here: every change gets tested on my own work before it ships, and keeping it to one author is part of why these are easy to trust.

What helps most:

- **Something didn't work:** open a [bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug.md) with what you asked and what you got.
- **An idea for a skill or a change:** open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.md). If it ships, it goes in the changelog.
- **You want your own version:** fork it and change whatever you like. That's what the MIT license is for.
